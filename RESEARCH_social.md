# Two-Person Motivation — research → build rules (2026-06-01)

How the best apps do warm, "kinda-dating-but-it's-encouragement" two-person fitness
accountability — and the rules we follow for the shared panda + buddy layer.
(Full sourced findings in the session; condensed here for the builder.)

## The one decision that matters
**Use the "shared CELEBRATION" model (Finch / Apple Fitness default), NOT "shared FATE"
(Habitica boss / Forest).** One person's bad day must never harm the partner or the
shared character. Shared-fate guilt is the exact weaponization to avoid (Victoria is
image-sensitive / under-eats).

## Rules for the shared panda
- **It NEVER dies.** It can nap / get "cozy", and perks back instantly on any
  interaction. No reset-to-zero, no "you lost your N-day bond."
- **Fed by EITHER person's wins** + combined positivity. Thrives when both check in
  (special "you both showed up" glow). 
- **Droop is collective + anonymous**: only after *mutual* inactivity (~36–48h), framed
  as "resting / waiting for you both" — the partner can NEVER tell whose miss caused it.
- Generous grace window; a single missed day does nothing.

## Nudges
- **All-positive menu only** (cheer / thinking-of-you / let's-both-show-up / proud /
  flex). If the only actions are kind, it can't be weaponized (the Gas app lesson).
- **Framed as from the human, not the app.** Opt-in to send, frequency-capped.
- **NEVER auto-fire about a missed goal**, and no nudge that implies "you haven't logged."

## Buddy status card — presence + vibe, NEVER magnitude
- ✅ "showed up today", shared streak (forgiving), mood/one-word vibe, a chosen non-scale win.
- ❌ NEVER weight, weight change, calories in/out, deficit/surplus, BMI, or red/green over/under.
- Strong evidence: calorie/weight numerics + good/bad framing drive disordered eating;
  ~73% of an ED cohort felt MyFitnessPal contributed to their disorder. Victoria's data is
  structurally unshareable; her goals are **floors** ("ate enough"), never ceilings.

## Asymmetric goals (correct + safe)
- Bryan: metric-ish ("hit protein", "lifted", "ate enough to grow").
- Victoria: behavior/floor ("ate three meals", "nourishing breakfast", "moved joyfully").
- The panda treats a completed "ate enough" identically to a completed "lifted."

## Three delight rituals to build
1. **Daily dual check-in + synchronized reveal** — each logs a tiny intention + 1-word mood
   independently; when BOTH have, the card flips to reveal both at once + the panda celebrates.
   (Paired/Agapé anticipation × Finch mutual presence, zero numbers.)
2. **Togetherness milestones** (a week, a month) → cosmetic panda surprises (outfit, bamboo,
   a scene) — tied to togetherness/effort, NOT a perfect streak; survives missed days.
3. **Surprise "good-vibe" gifts** — send the other a sticker / heart / saved note the panda
   delivers. Occasional, warm — this is where the safe flirtiness lives.

## Grounding
Self-Determination Theory: support autonomy (own goals, opt-in nudges), competence
(celebrate their wins, no comparison), relatedness (shared panda, rituals). It should feel
like something you *get to* tend together — never something you're *afraid to* let down.
